IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH
1. Replace the sample services and session lengths with the business's real offerings.
2. Add the business's US city/state, business email, phone, operating hours, and policies.
3. The booking form is currently a front-end demo. It prepares a request text file locally and DOES NOT email or store bookings. Connect a secure form/booking provider before launch.
4. Online payments are NOT enabled yet. Connect a payment provider (for example Stripe or PayPal) using the business owner's own verified account and add the provider's checkout link/button. Never collect card details directly in this website form.
5. Confirm that the domain name is available before purchasing it. A domain purchase and hosting are separate from these website files.
6. Use real, properly licensed photos and confirm all business claims before publishing.

To preview
Open index.html in a browser. An internet connection is needed for the remote photos and Google Fonts.
