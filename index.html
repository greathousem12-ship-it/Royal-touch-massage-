Complete the appointment request form. Your request is not confirmed until the business contacts you.

Can I pay online?＋

Online checkout needs to be connected to the business's payment provider before payments can be accepted. Never enter card details into this form.

What if I'm not sure which service to choose?＋

Select “Not sure yet” and include your question in the notes. The therapist can help you understand the available options.

Is massage therapy right for everyone?＋

Not every technique is appropriate for every person. Share relevant concerns directly with a qualified therapist before your session and consult a healthcare professional when needed.
